Services

Design, development, supply, installation, software and service – we cover the entire life cycle of the equipment, or individual services separately according to the client's requirements.

01

Design

Separately or as part of turnkey supplies, we prepare design documentation for construction at all stages – from studies and investment plans, documentation for zoning and building permits and tender documentation for public procurement, to construction documentation and as-built documentation. Preparing operating regulations and operating manuals is also an integral part of our design team's work.

In accordance with the Building Act, we negotiate the documentation with utility network administrators and with persons or organisations whose approval of the proposed technical solution is required.

Electrical, I&C and process control designs are prepared by our own design department with authorised engineers; documentation for other professions is provided with the support of partner companies, with us acting as the general designer.

We prepare designs electronically using EPLAN, AUTOCAD, WORD and EXCEL, and we have our own high-performance reprographic equipment.

02

Development

Based on requests for non-standard solutions, or to meet individual customer wishes, we take part in the development of new devices, which are then verified in practical use. This also includes model workstations for verifying alternative process procedures and not yet proven operating methods, or alternative uses of modified process sensors and electronic devices for purposes other than those primarily intended by the manufacturer.

One result of our own development is FISHMONITOR, a device for continuous monitoring of drinking water quality based on computer evaluation of the activity of detection fish. In its extended version, the device can also wirelessly transmit images from the aquarium to control rooms or to any computer connected to the internet.

03

Supply

To deliver supplies based on our own or customer-provided designs, we have an implementation team that carries out general or final supplies and sub-supplies of construction projects in an engineering manner, based on contracts with selected subcontractors who have sufficient technical capacities and qualified specialists.

The implementation team also purchases process equipment and sensors from foreign or domestic suppliers and hands them over for installation on site.

04

Installation

With our own site coordinators we provide professional management of construction and installation work by our partner installation companies according to the approved design documentation and prepared construction schedules. During installation we coordinate with other project participants and, together with the design team, take the operational measures needed for successful completion of the installation work.

We complete the installation with individual tests, followed by comprehensive testing and handover of the work for trial operation. We hand the work over to the customer with complete documentation of the supplied equipment, the required inspection reports, as-built documentation and operating regulations.

05

Software

Our team of programmers within the implementation department develops control programs for the automation equipment used, creates visualisation programs for operator workstations and provides communication with other independent subsystems or the higher control level.

We program PLCs using the software of the individual PLC manufacturers. To display data on operator PCs we use visualisation software Siemens WinCC, Wonderware InTouch or Microsys Promotic. The choice of visualisation depends on the customer's wishes, the size of the application and the cost of the visualisation.

We design PLC systems as open, easily expandable systems able to communicate with the customer's other systems via a range of standard industrial protocols. We also transmit information from the controlled process not only to process PCs but also to computers in the local company network or the internet. We provide remote supervision with the option of remote software modifications via mobile networks.

We transmit process data and alarms from unmanned sites to central control rooms or to mobile phones via radio or mobile networks.

06

Service

We provide warranty and post-warranty service for all completed contracts, and we apply our knowledge of equipment diagnostics and parameterisation for customers also to familiar components supplied by third parties. We provide periodic inspections under contract and carry out pre-season and post-season checks on heating equipment.

07

Trading

An important part of our business is also trading in components of control systems, controllers, sensors, control valves and other instrumentation and control equipment at attractive prices for the customer, as well as providing expert assistance to external organisations.
