Designing automation components and supplying them to control entire plants, interconnected process units or individual machines and mechanisms using PLCs and operator and dispatcher workstations has been one of our core tasks since the company was founded. Machinery equipped with control technology supplied and commissioned by us can be found in every field we work in.

5.1

Control systems

We work mainly with Siemens SIMATIC programmable controllers, but at customers' request we have also delivered projects with Allen-Bradley, TECO and Mitsubishi PLCs.

Process sensors, process analysers and control actuators are sourced mainly from foreign companies such as Siemens, Hach-Lange, Krohne, Endress+Hauser, WTW and Rosemount, or from domestic suppliers such as JSP Nová Paka, BD Sensors and others. We consult the choice of sensors with the customer to meet their chosen standardisation. For economical speed control of drives we use frequency converters from proven manufacturers Siemens, Danfoss, ABB, Nord and Lenze.

We have implemented extensive control systems and control centres at EVRAZ VÍTKOVICE STEEL, in the breweries of Plzeňský Prazdroj a.s., at wastewater treatment plants of water companies, and at the sources and the central drinking water treatment plant for Ostrava operated by OVAK a.s.

For remote transmission we mainly use radio modems and GSM/GPRS modems by CONEL and RACOM.

5.2

Power distribution

Besides designing new power switchboards at various voltage levels, we reconstruct existing low and high voltage switchgear with minimal outages and without the need for long-term plant shutdown. The same applies to the reconstruction of control pulpits in engineering plants. We have reconstructed entire switchgear rooms at EVRAZ Vítkovice Steel and at the Central WWTP in Ostrava.

We have experience with surge protection, trace heating of process piping, etc.

5.3

Energy

We design and supply HV switchgear and transformer substations, including their connection to high-voltage supply systems. We can connect electricity generated by CHP units to the grid or provide backup power using battery emergency sources and diesel generators.

We supply power factor correction switchboards and, as part of control system supplies, we can provide quarter-hour peak demand monitoring.

We have delivered electrical designs for biogas plants (BGP) and final supplies including commissioning and remote administration of electrical equipment, process sensors and control systems for biogas plants in the Czech Republic, Spain and Poland.

5.4

Building electrical installations

As part of our contracts we also prepare design documentation for the electrical installations of operating buildings, office buildings, halls and workshops, and then supply and install them accordingly. We take the same approach to outdoor lighting. Interior and exterior lighting and internal power distribution designed by us can be found, for example, in the Vinohradská 230 office complex in Prague and in newly built wastewater treatment plants, water treatment plants and reconstructed facilities.
