Metallurgy and engineering

Electrical, I&C and control systems for secondary steel refining, rolling mills and engineering plants.

— specialisations40 reference projects

On this page

  • Selected references
Send an inquiry

As part of modernising process technologies in metallurgical production, steelworks and related engineering plants, we mainly supply electrical, instrumentation & control equipment and control systems for secondary (ladle) steel refining as well as rolling mills and rolled material finishing lines.

Major contracts in our history include supplies for new vacuum degassing stations and secondary metallurgy equipment for the EVRAZ VÍTKOVICE STEEL and Huta Częstochowa (Poland) steelworks, and the deployment of an extensive distributed control system at EVRAZ VÍTKOVICE STEEL for the finishing line of the 3.5 KVARTO plate mill, carried out together with the reconstruction of control pulpits and power switchgear rooms.

We also took part in building continuous casting and secondary metallurgy equipment at ArcelorMittal Ostrava, and we cooperate closely with VÚHŽ a.s. Dobrá, to whom we supply software for industrial wire and cored-wire feeders and argon stirring equipment for domestic and foreign steelworks.

Projects delivered according to our designs and using control programs developed by us can also be found at Třinecké železárny a.s., Železiarne Podbrezová and OFZ, a.s. Istebné.
