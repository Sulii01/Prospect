In water management technologies our activities cover water sources, water treatment plants, pumping stations and distribution systems for drinking water and industrial process water. We are also significantly involved in the design and construction of sewage pumping stations and wastewater treatment plants operated via water management control centres.

1.1

Water sources and water treatment plants

We have helped to implement pumping from boreholes and wells and subsequent water treatment using wireless communication via water control centres we built at the wellfields and treatment plants of the Pilsner Urquell (Plzeň), Radegast and Velké Popovice breweries. We also provide controlled pumping from local sources and automation of water treatment for Ostravské vodárny a kanalizace a.s.

With distributed control systems interconnected by radio modems and GSM/GPRS modems, the available drinking water sources are used optimally and the treated water is stored in reservoirs.

The water treatment technologies we work with also include reverse osmosis, preparation and dosing of lime milk, sand filtration, chlorination systems, etc.

1.2

Drinking water pumping stations and distribution

Supply and control of pumping station complexes and automatic booster stations (ATS) for delivering water to consumers are also part of our turnkey supplies. We are a major supplier of booster stations for the City of Ostrava and take part in the reconstruction of technically obsolete pressure stations in residential areas and in their remote monitoring using modern wireless communication technologies.

Using automation technology, we also provide distribution of treated water with optimised utilisation in the breweries of the Plzeňský Prazdroj a.s. group and from the sources of the water company OVAK a.s.

1.3

Process water pumping stations and distribution

We provide pumping of process water into the cooling circuits of thermally stressed parts, especially in metallurgical plants, using automation technology and measuring instruments – for example in secondary (ladle) steel treatment at the EVRAZ VÍTKOVICE STEEL and Huta Częstochowa steelworks. We implemented control of the fire water supply for the Pilsner Urquell brewery. In all cases this is done by industrial control systems linked to the higher level of plant control.

1.4

Wastewater treatment plants and sewage pumping stations

We take part in the reconstruction of existing and construction of new wastewater treatment plants (WWTPs) for major water companies such as OVAK, SmVaK Ostrava, VaK Vsetín and VaK Krnov, as well as for smaller municipal WWTPs of individual towns or factories. On some contracts we participate only in the design phase, on others we act as the supplier of complete parts of the WWTP and the related pumping stations on sewer mains.

We also carried out the reconstruction of the electrical part and deployment of control systems at the WWTPs of the Pilsner Urquell and Velké Popovice breweries.

With final supplies of electrical equipment and control systems we take part in the reconstruction of existing WWTPs in Poland and the construction of new WWTPs in Poland, Bulgaria, Lithuania and Croatia.
