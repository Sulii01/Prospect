Our contribution to the efficient and economical use of heat sources is the supply of heating plant equipment, controlled almost exclusively by SIEMENS control technology, building on the proven foundations of the original instrumentation & control (I&C) components and sensors by LANDIS & GYR, later LANDIS & STAEFA.

Together with the reconstruction of boiler rooms, heat exchanger and transfer stations and the installation of high-performance air conditioning and HVAC equipment, we also set up new control room workstations for the operators, equipped with computers and visualisation software.

2.1

Boiler rooms and heat exchanger stations

Ever since solid-fuel boiler rooms began to be replaced by gas-fired heat sources, we have been designing and supplying instrumentation and control systems for central and local boiler rooms. From supplying only I&C and electrical equipment we gradually moved on to supplying complete boiler room, heat exchanger station and substation technologies. Major heating projects with our participation include the heating systems of Darkov Spa in Karviná, the Hrabyně Rehabilitation Institute, the Siemens Electric Motors plant in Mohelnice, Jeseník Hospital, and heat exchanger stations in Ostrava, Bohumín, Orlová, Krnov, etc.

Besides these large projects, we also supply heating technology for medium and small boiler rooms of schools, public offices and residential buildings.

2.2

Air conditioning equipment

Together with control technology for heat sources, we supply air conditioning equipment for all kinds of commercial, residential and production spaces. We have supplied I&C equipment and control systems for the surgical wards of hospitals in Vsetín and Valašské Meziříčí, the air conditioning system of Komerční banka, Česká spořitelna and the Regional Court in Ostrava, as well as the operating building and ceremonial halls of the Ostrava crematorium.

We control industrial air conditioning and ventilation equipment in OKD mining plants as well as in the ČEZ Aréna sports hall in Ostrava.
