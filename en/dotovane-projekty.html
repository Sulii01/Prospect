Special controlled rotary drives for industrial units

CZ.01.1.02/0.0/0.0/16_084/0010144

The subject of the project is industrial research and experimental development of motion control of rotary drives of industrial equipment at a set speed, with the possibility of changing and synchronising them within the system, for optimal conditions of the production process. The main result of the project will be a prototype and utility model of a Mini digital control unit enabling precise setting of production process parameters with maximum electricity savings.

It is co-financed by the European Union.
