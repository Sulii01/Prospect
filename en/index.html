About us

From design to remote monitoring. Since 1991.

PROSPECT spol. s r.o. operates as an engineering and design company in the field of investment construction, focused on turnkey supplies of automated process systems, building services, electrical equipment, instrumentation and control equipment and control systems.

PROSPECT spol. s r.o. was founded in 1991 and is registered in the Commercial Register at the Regional Court in Ostrava. Our structure of permanent employees, external specialists and proven partner companies enables us to offer and provide comprehensive services for project delivery – from the preparation of design documentation all the way to regular post-warranty inspections and maintenance of the operated equipment, including its remote administration and monitoring via radio or mobile networks and the internet.

“The company's philosophy is based on a professional approach in technical matters and fairness in business relationships.”

The company focuses mainly on the following fields:

  • Water management

  • Gas equipment
  • Process control & electrical

Control systems & software

At the heart of every technology is a control system.

We work mainly with Siemens SIMATIC programmable controllers, but at customers' request we have also delivered projects with Allen-Bradley, TECO and Mitsubishi PLCs.

We design PLC systems as open, easily expandable systems able to communicate with the customer's other systems via a range of standard industrial protocols. We also transmit information from the controlled process not only to process PCs but also to computers in the local company network or the internet. We provide remote supervision with the option of remote software modifications via mobile networks.

We have implemented extensive control systems and control centres at EVRAZ VÍTKOVICE STEEL, in the breweries of Plzeňský Prazdroj a.s., at wastewater treatment plants of water companies, and at the sources and the central drinking water treatment plant for Ostrava operated by OVAK a.s.

The professional profile of our staff primarily covers the needs of our own general or final construction supplies, as well as the delivery of contracts based on design documentation provided by the customer.

In addition to design work, the activities of PROSPECT spol. s r.o. include developing user programs for control systems including their visualisation, technical supervision during construction, individual and comprehensive testing of the supplied equipment, preparation of inspection reports and operating manuals, operator training, and warranty and post-warranty service.

We also provide these services separately, according to the individual requirements of the client. Complete supplies covering civil, process and electrical works are delivered together with our long-term subcontractors with sufficient production and installation capacities.

Throughout the company's existence we have regularly prepared design documentation for more than 100 projects a year, and we subsequently deliver almost half of them ourselves.
